## 2024-03-20 - Pygame Video Frame Surface Conversion
**Learning:** Using `pygame.image.frombuffer(array.tobytes(), (width, height), 'RGB')` is extremely fast (10-15x faster than the `pygame.surfarray.make_surface` + `np.transpose` approach). Also, using `cv2.resize` directly on the `ndarray` *before* color conversion (`cv2.cvtColor`) and surface creation minimizes pixel processing and is measurably faster. Always resize frames natively with OpenCV before converting color spaces or Pygame surfaces to minimize overhead.
**Action:** When bridging OpenCV to Pygame, always resize in OpenCV (BGR), convert to RGB, make contiguous, and use `pygame.image.frombuffer`.
