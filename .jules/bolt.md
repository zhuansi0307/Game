## 2024-05-24 - Pygame Video Rendering Zero-Copy Optimization
**Learning:** Pygame `surfarray.make_surface` coupled with `np.transpose` introduces a massive CPU/memory overhead when repeatedly rendering video frames, easily tanking frame rates. `pygame.image.frombuffer` offers a near zero-copy mechanism, but requires passing the size tuple correctly as `(width, height)` rather than the default numpy `(height, width)`. Furthermore, native OpenCV resizing (`cv2.resize`) before creating the surface is significantly faster than using `pygame.transform.smoothscale`.
**Action:** When rendering high-frequency video frames from OpenCV to Pygame, always convert to RGB, resize using OpenCV (`cv2.resize`), ensure the array is contiguous (`np.ascontiguousarray`), and construct the surface via `pygame.image.frombuffer(frame.data, (width, height), 'RGB')` to completely avoid expensive copying and transposition.
