## 2025-02-12 - Optimize Pygame video rendering from OpenCV
**Learning:** `pygame.surfarray.make_surface` + `pygame.transform.smoothscale` is extremely slow compared to `cv2.resize` + `pygame.image.frombuffer`. In `engine/cutscene_player.py`'s synchronous video frame fallback, `pygame.transform.smoothscale` combined with `np.transpose` makes video reading terribly slow (3.9s vs 0.1s for 100 frames). Even in `engine/video_frame_reader.py` (the preloading thread), `np.transpose` + `pygame.surfarray.make_surface` is about 5x slower (0.55s vs 0.1s for 100 frames) than using zero-copy `pygame.image.frombuffer`.
**Action:** Replace `pygame.surfarray.make_surface` and `pygame.transform.smoothscale` for video frames with `cv2.resize(frame)` + `pygame.image.frombuffer(rgb.data, (rgb.shape[1], rgb.shape[0]), 'RGB')`. Note that `np.ascontiguousarray()` should be called after modifying the ndarray, before passing to `frombuffer`.
