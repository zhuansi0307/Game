## 2023-10-27 - [Optimize Pygame Video Frame Rendering]
**Learning:** For Pygame + OpenCV video frame rendering, `pygame.surfarray.make_surface` and `np.transpose` are very slow (10.6ms/frame). Pygame 2.x supports `pygame.image.frombuffer` which avoids memory copies, offering a 32x speedup (0.3ms/frame). When resizing, `cv2.resize` combined with `frombuffer` (0.11ms) is 14x faster than `pygame.surfarray.make_surface` + `pygame.transform.smoothscale` (1.7ms).
**Action:** Always prefer `pygame.image.frombuffer(frame.data, (width, height), 'RGB')` with contiguous ndarrays directly mapped to Pygame Surfaces, rather than explicitly converting using `np.transpose` and `pygame.surfarray.make_surface` in Pygame codebases handling high frame rate OpenCV videos. Avoid `pygame.transform.smoothscale` for ndarrays in favor of `cv2.resize`.
